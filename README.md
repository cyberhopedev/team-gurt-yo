# team-gurt-yo
Repository for visual game project made for NCSU CSC281

Vampire Haven is a 2d turn-based rpg game in which the player must find their way out of a town of vampire, by fighting through the dungeons of the seven sins. Use the rewards from your battles to purchase items and stats to become even stronger for future fights. Use the blood vial from an unlikely ally to use the powers of the vampire to assist you in battle, and watch the gameplay as well as yourself change.

## Features

(List features of the game)

-Turn-based combat

-Rpg elements including classes, items, stats, and npcs

-Story in which the characters effect your gameplay and can assist you with your progress

-Simple 2d pixel art style
 
## Gameplay Flow
 
Choose a class and fight your way through the dungeons, getting rewards and perks that make you stronger. Use your gold and interact with the world's characterss in order to progress through the story and level up your gear to escape the town.
